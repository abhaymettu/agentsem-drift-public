# Do the observer-step claims replicate on fresh populations?

**Status: DESIGN STAGE, EXPLORATORY. Every number is synthetic. No prediction is scored.** This checks whether
the claims STEP-003, UNLOG-003 and BREAK-003 drew from one simulated population hold in new ones, and whether
their person-bootstrap intervals describe population-to-population variation. It decides nothing and changes no
spec.

Code: `sim/rep_003.py` -> `sim/rep_003.json`. `phi_D` 0.7, 28-day baseline, W 14, NET-003's DR+PS pair,
in-window persons, detectors and tuning (`tuning_003.json`) as in BREAK-003. Every imported file is unchanged.

## Why

NET-003 through BREAK-003 (next10 to next17) all ran on one population: design seed 20261003, N 1000, 247
in-window persons, one resample matrix. Each phase chose its question after reading the previous phase's numbers.
So the observer-step story the paper would tell (L1_did's transfer cost, L1_step's gain growing with rho, the
w/p break-even) is an adaptive chain fitted to one draw. Two things are unknown:
1. Whether the claims hold in fresh populations from the same generator, or partly reflect the design draw.
2. Whether the person-bootstrap intervals used throughout are calibrated. The generator draws every random
   quantity per person (AR(1) panels, loadings `lam`, events, usage), so persons are independent and the
   bootstrap should be roughly right. That has not been checked, and p* is clipped at 0 and 1, so its percentile
   intervals can be degenerate (BREAK-003 reports [1, 1] cells).

A simulation study for the paper needs this anyway: point estimates with Monte Carlo standard errors over
replicate populations, not one draw (Morris, White & Crowther 2019, ADEMP).

## Plan (committed before the run)

**Populations.** R = 200 fresh populations, seed base r = first 8 hex digits of sha256("rep003:r"), r = 0..199,
asserted distinct and disjoint from the design (20261003), tuning (20260927) and check (20261004) seeds. A
PREREG-003 evaluation seed is a hash of PREREG-003.md, which does not exist; no evaluation seed is touched. Each
population has its own N 1000 persons, own in-window set and own resample matrix `default_rng([seed, 99])`, as
the design run does.

**Quantities, per population**, for rho in {0, 0.5, 1, 2}, shift arms `conc0.5`, `conc-0.5`, `post0.5` (in the
D arm and the no-D-change arm), detectors k 0.5 and k*, horizons 21, 42, 63 d: BREAK-003's T0, F0, Ts, Fs,
U(w, p) for w in {1, 2, 4} and p in {0, 1} (U is linear in p), and p*(w); plus UNLOG-003's L1_did - L1_step
added false-alarm share per arm (no rho). Each as point, bootstrap SE and 95% percentile interval (1000
resamples). U(1, 0) is STEP-003 part A's D-dependent L1_step - L1.

**Reproduction check.** The design seed runs through the same code and must reproduce `break_003.json` (all 936
values) and `unlog_003.json` (L1_did - L1_step added, 12 values) to within 1e-4 per est/se/lo/hi; the bootstrap is
vectorized, so float order differs in the last bits. Run once on the design seed before this commit (948 values,
pass); no fresh population was generated before the commit.

**Per quantity, across the 200 populations:** mean and Monte Carlo SE, between-population SD, mean bootstrap SE,
SD / mean SE, coverage (share of populations whose own 95% interval contains the 200-population mean), share of
points > 0, share of intervals above 0 and below 0, 5/50/95% quantiles, and the design value's percentile.

**Claims, fixed now from the committed docs** (k 0.5, 63 d, in-window, `conc0.5` unless stated; est = point,
lo = interval lower end). Each is scored as the share of the 200 populations in which it holds. All 11 hold in
the design population.

## Expectations (stated before the run)

Reasoning from the design values and their bootstrap SEs, taking the design draw as typical:
1. **Calibration.** Over the continuous quantities (T0, F0, Ts, Fs, U; all 72 cells), median coverage is in
   [0.93, 0.97] and median SD / mean SE in [0.9, 1.1], because persons are independent within a population.
2. **p\* intervals are not calibrated.** p*'s coverage is below 0.90 in at least one k 0.5, 63 d `conc0.5` cell
   (clipping at 1 makes [1, 1] intervals).
3. **The design draw is typical.** Of the 44 primary values (k 0.5, 63 d, `conc0.5`; T0, F0, Ts, Fs, U(1, 0),
   U(1, 1), U(2, 1), U(4, 1), p*(1), p*(2), p*(4) at 4 rho), at least 40 have a design percentile in [0.05, 0.95].
4. **Claim replication.** C1, C2, C3, C7a, C7b hold in at least 90% of populations (margins of 3 or more SEs, or
   paired across rho). C4a, C4b, C6 hold in at least 70%. C4c and C5 hold in under 90% (rho 0 U(1, 1) -0.028,
   SE 0.029; rho 2 U(2, 1) -0.028, SE 0.063). C8 holds in under 70% (rho 0, w 1: 0.004, SE 0.025).
