# An implementable fix for L1's ramp absorption: re-estimate only the observer step

**Status: DESIGN STAGE, EXPLORATORY. Every number is synthetic. No prediction is scored.** This informs how
PREREG-003 and the paper could specify the re-baseline after a logged observer change. It decides nothing and
changes no spec.

Code: `sim/obs_003.py` -> `sim/obs_003.json`. Design seed 20261003, `phi_D` 0.7, 28-day baseline, the design
run's resample matrix, NET-003's paired arms (DR+PS, and the same persons with `delta_D = 0`).
`study003.py`, `decide_003.py`, `net_003.py`, `absorb_003.py`, `drift_sim.py` and spec 003 are unchanged.

## Why

ABSORB-003: at k 0.5, W 14, in-window DR+PS persons (n = 247), L1's 63-day D-dependent sensitivity is 0.190; with
the D ramp removed from its re-baseline (L1_bothCF, oracle information) it is 0.348, and the oracle rule's is
0.441. The mean channel carries the gain. Every variant that fixed it used the no-D-change arm, which exists only
in simulation; keeping the baseline SD did nothing. Its proposal 2 left one implementable fix untested: estimate
only the observer step, on the mapped indicators, and leave the person's change in the scores.

## Plan (committed before the run)

The logged change in DR+PS is an intercept step on the mapped indicators 1, 2 and 6; D loads on all six. The new
rules keep the detector's 28-day baseline mean and SD and, after the hold, subtract an estimated step from the
mapped indicators' baseline z. The hold, reason 4 and (for L1) the S reset on `c + 1` are the base rule's.

| rule | mapped indicators after the hold | unmapped after the hold | base |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | own hold mean and SD | own hold mean and SD | the rule (reproduces NET-003) |
| L1_step | baseline z minus their hold-mean z | baseline z | L1 |
| L1_did | baseline z minus (their hold-mean z minus the unmapped indicators' mean hold z) | baseline z | L1 |
| L2 | own hold mean and SD | baseline z (never touched) | the rule (reproduces NET-003) |
| L2_did | as L1_did | baseline z | L2 |
| L1_bothCF | ABSORB-003's ramp-free L1 (oracle information) | | reference ceiling |

All but L1_bothCF are implementable: they use the log, its indicator map and the person's own observed scores.
L1_did is a within-person difference in differences: the unmapped indicators' hold shift reads the person's
concurrent change where the logged change does not act, and is subtracted from each mapped indicator's shift. It
assumes D moves the indicators by about the same amount in baseline z, which the generator does not guarantee
(loadings U[0.25, 0.5] per person and indicator, and indicator SDs differ). With fewer than 2 hold days on every
unmapped indicator the reference is 0 and L1_did falls back to L1_step.

Map error arm: the log map misses indicator 6 (`log_map = (1, 2)`), which the change does shift. Then indicator 6
is neither held (L2) nor stepped, and it contaminates L1_did's reference. Run for L2 and L1_did.

For W in (14, 21), detectors A-fixed `k = 0.5` and `k*`, horizons 21, 42 and 63 days, in-window (n = 247) and all
logged persons:
1. Each rule's raw sensitivity, no-change rate and D-dependent sensitivity (NET-003's measures).
2. Paired differences of D-dependent sensitivity: L1_did - L1, L2_did - L2, L1_did_om6 - L1.
3. Step-estimate error on the mapped indicators (estimate minus the true step 0.5 / baseline SD, in-window persons,
   point mean and SD), raw and DiD, on both arms.

Asserts: L0, L1, L2 and the oracle rule reproduce `net_003.json` (raw, no-change rate, D-dependent; both
populations) at every W, detector and horizon; L1_bothCF reproduces `absorb_003.json`; the log is exogenous with
map (1, 2, 6).

Expected (k 0.5, W 14, in-window, 63 days unless stated):
1. The asserts pass.
2. L1_did - L1 is positive, its interval excludes 0, and it is at least half of ABSORB-003's L1_bothCF - L1
   (0.158), so 0.079 or more.
3. L1_step lies between L1 and L1_did (point values).
4. Step error on the DR+PS arm: the raw estimate's mean error is positive, and DiD cuts its absolute value by at
   least half. On the no-D-change arm both mean errors are within 0.05 of 0, and DiD's error SD is larger.
5. L1_did's no-change rate is at least L1's and at most the oracle rule's plus 0.03.
6. L2_did - L2 is positive as a point estimate; its interval covers 0.
7. Map error: L2_om6's no-change rate is above L2's, and L1_did_om6 - L1 stays positive as a point estimate.
8. Among all logged persons (most have onset outside the window), L1_did - L1 at 63 days is not negative as a
   point estimate: the fix does not cost persons whose change does not overlap the hold.
