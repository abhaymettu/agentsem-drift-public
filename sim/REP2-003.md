# REP2-003: generator means for the still single-draw observer-step results

**Status: DESIGN STAGE, EXPLORATORY. SYNTHETIC ONLY. Every number is synthetic. No prediction is scored.** This
reruns, on REP-003's 200 populations, the results that OBSERVER-STEP-DIGEST.md section 2 lists as single-draw,
so the paper can cite a generator mean with its Monte Carlo SE instead of one design draw. It decides nothing and
changes no spec.

Code: `sim/rep2_003.py` -> `sim/rep2_003.json`. `phi_D` 0.7, 28-day baseline, W 14, NET-003's DR+PS pair (the D
arm and the same persons with `delta_D = 0`), A-fixed k 0.5, in-window persons (onset in `[c, c + 13]`),
`exclude_pre_onset_alarms=False`, detectors and tuning (`tuning_003.json`) as in the source scripts. Every
imported file is unchanged.

## Why

REP-003 replicated STEP-003 part A, UNLOG-003's L1_did - L1_step and BREAK-003 on 200 fresh populations and
found that the design draw was not typical at rho 0: F0 (L1_step - L1 no-change rate) sat at the 15.5th
percentile and offset every U(w, 0) by 0.024 w, and STEP-003's rho-0 gain of 0.049 was 0.004 in the generator.
The digest's section 2 results come from the same design population (seed 20261003, 247 in-window persons) and
several share F0's draw (the L1_did - L1 and HYB-003 step 3 values contain a no-change-rate term). They are
still single-draw.

## Plan (committed before the run)

r = 0..199, asserted there to be distinct and disjoint from the design, tuning and check seeds). No new seed.
Each population is built as `rep_003.population` builds it: `n3.SEED = seed`, `n3.drps_pair()`, its own
in-window set. FACT-003's two extra arms come from `fact_003.arms()` with `f3.SEED = seed`. No bootstrap matrix is
drawn (points only, below). The design seed runs first through the same code.

## Expectations (stated before the run)

Reasoning from the design values and their bootstrap SEs, and from REP-003: the design's F0 (L1_step - L1
no-change rate) was -0.093 against a generator -0.069, and T0 sat at its 70th to 80th percentile for rho up to 1.
A contrast that contains a no-change-rate difference involving L1's re-baseline inherits that draw; a contrast
between two arms of the same panel with equal no-change rates does not. Scored from `summary`, k 0.5, 63 d unless
stated.
1. **FACT.** The L0 interaction (21 d) has a generator mean within 0.06 of the design's 0.154 (about 1.6 design
   SEs of 0.037; L0 has no re-baseline, so F0's draw does not enter), and `design_side` (> 0) is at least 0.95.
2. **ABSORB.** L1_bothCF - L1 has a generator mean within 0.045 of the design's 0.158 (about 2 design SEs of
   0.023; both variants have the same no-change rate by construction, so F0's draw cancels), and `design_side`
   is at least 0.95.
3. **OBS/LOAD, rho 0.5 to 2.** L1_did - L1 has a generator mean below the design value at rho 0.5, 1 and 2 (design
   0.105, 0.194, 0.316). Reason: in the design L1_did and L1_step have the same no-change rate (0.211), so L1_did -
   L1 carries the same F0 offset (0.024) as STEP-003 part A, plus T0's draw at rho up to 1. `design_side` (> 0) is
   at least 0.95 at rho 1 and 2 and at least 0.80 at rho 0.5.
4. **OBS/LOAD, rho 0.** The rho-0 reading is design-draw sensitive, as REP-003's was: the generator mean of
   L1_did - L1 at rho 0 is within 0.03 of 0 (design 0.012, SE 0.028), and `design_side` (> 0) is below 0.75.
5. **UNLOG.** The generator means order L1 < L1_step < L2 < L1_did and L1 < oracle < L2 (design 0.004, 0.283,
   0.397, 0.482; oracle 0.300), each of the five means is within 0.05 of its design value, and L1's mean is within
   0.02 of 0. The L1_step vs oracle order (design gap 0.017) is not predicted.
6. **HYB step 3.** D-dependent step 3 rises with rho in the generator: the mean at rho 2 exceeds the mean at rho 0
   by at least 0.10 (design 0.162), and the rho-0 mean is below the design's 0.040 and within 0.025 of 0 (design-
   draw sensitive at rho 0, as item 4). The no-change step 3 has a generator mean in [-0.075, -0.030] (design
   -0.069 carries part of F0's draw, about three quarters of it if step 3's design share of L1_step - L1 holds)
   with `design_side` (< 0) at least 0.90.

## Runtime

Measured on the smoke test (`python3 sim/rep2_003.py --smoke --workers 2`, design seed plus 2 populations, run
after the expectations above were written; machine load average about 9.5 on 12 cores): 3.9 s wall, 1.8 to
2.0 s per population (mean 1.9). Points only, no bootstrap, which is why this is far cheaper than REP-003. The
full run is 201 population calls: 201 x 1.9 / 8, about 50 s at 8 workers, plus pool start-up; allow 1 to 3
minutes under load.

## What this does not do

- No new seeds: the 200 populations are REP-003's, imported; the design seed is used only for the reproduction
  check.
- No paired intervals beyond what REP-003 computed: each population contributes a point only. There is no
  per-population bootstrap and no calibration check of the section 2 intervals (the digest notes they were not
  checked; this run leaves that open). Between-population SD and MCSE are reported instead.
- No other cell: only k 0.5, 63 d (21 d for FACT), W 14, the digest's rows. No k*, no W 21, no other horizon, arm
  or rule pair.
- No change to any committed note or script. STEP-003, BREAK-003, HYB-003, the digest and the source scripts stay
  as committed; source code is imported, and the inline contrasts are re-expressed in `rep2_003.py` with their
  line numbers named.
- It does not choose L1, L1_step or L1_did, or the false-alarm weight; those stay his.
