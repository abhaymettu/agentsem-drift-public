# Which part of L1's re-baseline does what: a chain from L1 to L1_step

**Status: DESIGN STAGE, EXPLORATORY. Every number is synthetic. No prediction is scored.** This informs which
observer-step rule, if any, PREREG-003 and the paper specify. It decides nothing and changes no spec.

Code: `sim/hyb_003.py` -> `sim/hyb_003.json`. Design seed 20261003, `phi_D` 0.7, 28-day baseline, W 14, the
design run's resample matrix, NET-003's DR+PS pair (the D arm and the same persons with `delta_D = 0`).
`step_003.py`, `obs_003.py`, `load_003.py`, `unlog_003.py` and everything they import are unchanged.

## Why

STEP-003 found that neither L1 nor L1_step dominates: L1 absorbs a concurrent unlogged unmapped shift, L1_step
keeps more D-dependent sensitivity, and L1_step - L1 grows with D's effect on the unmapped indicators (rho). It
left three items open:
- the mechanism behind that growth with rho, which it did not isolate;
- a hybrid rule (re-baseline the unmapped indicators as L1 does, subtract the raw step on the mapped ones as
  L1_step does), untested;
- from OBS-003 (next12): which part of L1's re-baseline raises its no-change alarm rate (k 0.5, 63 d: L1 0.304,
  L1_step 0.211).

L1 and L1_step differ in three separable ways after the hold. L1 re-baselines every indicator to its hold mean
and hold SD. L1_step keeps the baseline mean and SD, subtracts the raw hold-mean step on the mapped indicators,
and leaves the unmapped indicators on their baseline z. On the mapped indicators both subtract the same hold
mean, up to the hold-day mask (L1: logged-use days; L1_step: usable days); the difference there is mainly the
SD. So:
1. mapped indicators: hold SD vs baseline SD;
2. unmapped indicators: hold SD vs baseline SD;
3. unmapped indicators: re-centred on the hold mean or not.

## Plan (committed before the run)

Rules, a chain in which each rule changes one of those three things from the one before it. All keep L1's hold,
reason 4 and S reset.
- **L1**: as specified.
- **L1_hyb** (the proposed hybrid): mapped indicators take the baseline z minus the raw hold-mean step
  (L1_step's treatment); unmapped keep L1's re-baseline.
- **L1_bsd**: every indicator takes the baseline z minus its raw hold-mean step. That is L1's re-centring with
  the baseline SD.
- **L1_step**: as in OBS-003.

Paired contrasts: L1_hyb - L1 (step 1), L1_bsd - L1_hyb (step 2), L1_step - L1_bsd (step 3), and L1_bsd - L1
(steps 1 and 2 together, the candidate against L1). The three steps sum to L1_step - L1 (asserted on the points).

Detectors A-fixed `k = 0.5` (primary) and `k*`; horizons 21, 42 and 63 days from t0; in-window persons
(n = 247).
- **Part 0.** No-change alarm share (no D change, no shift) per rule, and the contrasts in it.
- **Part A.** LOAD-003's rho sweep (0, 0.5, 1, 2): D-dependent share per rule and the contrasts.
- **Part B.** STEP-003's unlogged unmapped shift arms (`conc0.25`, `conc0.5`, `post0.5`, `conc-0.5`), no D
  change: added false-alarm share per rule and the contrasts.
- **Part C.** STEP-003's part C (the shift in both the D arm and the no-D-change arm; `conc0.5`, `conc-0.5`,
  `post0.5`): `dd`, added, and `trade` = `dd` - added (1:1, a CHOICE; the paper's weighting is the owner's). The
  trade leaves out the no-change rate, which part 0 reports.

Asserts: L1 and L1_step reproduce `obs_003.json` (no-change share), `load_003.json` (D-dependent share, every
rho) and `step_003.json` (part B added share, part C `dd`), both detectors, 3 horizons.

Expected (k 0.5, in-window, 63 days unless stated):
1. The asserts pass.
2. Part 0: L1 - L1_bsd in no-change share is positive with an interval excluding 0, and its point is at least
   half of L1 - L1_step (0.093). That is, the hold SD, not the re-centring, is the main source of L1's higher
   no-change rate.
3. Part A: step 3 (L1_step - L1_bsd) grows with rho: its point at rho 2 exceeds its point at rho 0 by at least
   0.10. Its interval covers 0 at rho 0 and excludes 0 at rho 2. Across rho, the range of its points is larger
   than the range of step 1's and of step 2's. That is, STEP-003 finding 1's growth with rho comes from
   re-centring the unmapped indicators.
4. Part B: L1_hyb's and L1_bsd's added share in `conc0.5` and `conc-0.5` are each within 0.03 of L1's (they
   re-centre the unmapped indicators, as L1 does). Step 3 in `conc0.5` added share is positive with an interval
   excluding 0.
5. Part C: L1_bsd - L1 `trade` is positive (point) in all three arms, and none of its 18 cells (3 arms, 2
   detectors, 3 horizons) has an interval entirely below 0.
6. Part C: step 3 `trade` is negative in `conc0.5` and positive in `post0.5` (points). That is, the L1 vs
   L1_step trade-off of STEP-003 finding 3 survives between L1_bsd and L1_step.
7. Part A, rho 1: L1_bsd - L1 in D-dependent share is positive (point).

Reading (not an expectation): if 2, 5 and 7 hold, L1_bsd is at least as good as L1 on every measure here. The
paper's remaining choice is then L1_bsd vs L1_step, which reduces to one question: re-centre the unmapped
indicators at a logged change or not. That trades absorbing unlogged changes that coincide with logged ones
against absorbing D's own early effect on the unmapped indicators.
